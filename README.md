## 🕶️ What is BillBlaze?
BillBlaze™ isn’t your dad’s Excel sheet.  
It’s **Word + Excel fused into a single beast** — designed to **create, track, and weaponize your business data**.  

- 🧾 Forge invoices & receipts like it’s second nature  
- 📊 Slice through revenue and profit stats (monthly, yearly)  
- ⚡ See what’s owed, what’s settled, and who’s still hanging in the shadows  
- 🗂 Summon your bills by category: *Unpaid, Owed, Settled, All*  

It’s not about managing data.  
It’s about owning it.

## 🔥 Features That Bite
- **Layouts That Obey You**  
  - Start with a raw layout or load a pre-made `.bbc` file  
  - Every layout carries a `sheetType` (taxInvoice, creditNote, debitNote, proformaInvoice, billOfSupply, none)  
  - Required fields keep analytics ruthless & precise (invoice #, date, payable, profits, etc.)  
  - Export bills to PDF, or stash them as `.bbc` files for later ambush  

- **Analytics with Teeth**  
  - Track revenue/profits by time, by bill type, or by mood  
  - Totals, unpaid revenue, unpaid counts — all laid bare  
  - Change your storage directory → BillBlaze migrates everything automatically  
  - The numbers don’t lie. But they can sting.  

- **Files That Follow Your Rules**  
  - Stored locally, under your chosen directory  
  - Cloud option? Yeah, but on **your** drive, not someone else’s  
  - `.bbc` files dropped in → instantly show up inside the app  
  - Register `.bbc` as the default → double-click from anywhere and it bends to BillBlaze
